# Support SLA & Escalation Matrix

Acme Software Support Operations (v4.1)

## Response time targets

- Urgent (production down, security): 1 hour, 24/7.
- High (blocked work, billing disputes): 4 business hours.
- Medium (questions, how-to): 1 business day.
- Low (feature requests, feedback): 3 business days.

## Escalation rules

Tickets involving refunds of $500 or more, account cancellations of enterprise
accounts, legal threats, or press inquiries must be escalated to a human
manager before any commitment is made to the customer.

## Auto-response policy

AI-drafted responses are permitted for duplicate charges under $500, password
reset guidance, standard troubleshooting, and pricing questions. Any response
promising a monetary adjustment above $500 requires human approval.

## Quality requirements

Every customer-facing response must cite or follow published policy. Responses
must not contain other customers' personal data. Complaints about repeated
issues are handled by senior staff only.
