# Pricing & Plans

Acme Software Pricing (v2.0)

## Plans

- Starter: $29/user/month, core features, email support.
- Growth: $79/user/month, advanced analytics, priority email support.
- Enterprise: custom pricing, SSO, dedicated CSM, 24/7 support, custom SLA.

## Discounts

Annual billing saves 2 months (roughly 17%). Registered nonprofits receive
40% off any plan. Volume discounts start at 50 seats.

## Trials

Growth plans include a 14-day free trial. Trials do not require a credit
card. Trial extensions of up to 14 additional days can be granted by support
one time per account as goodwill.

## Billing

Subscriptions are billed in advance. Upgrades are prorated immediately;
downgrades take effect at the next renewal. Invoices are available in the
billing portal and emailed to billing contacts.
