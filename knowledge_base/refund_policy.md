# Refund Policy

Acme Software Refund Policy (v3.2, effective January 2026)

## Duplicate charges

If a customer is charged twice for the same subscription period, the duplicate
charge is fully refundable. Refunds under $500 are processed automatically by
the billing system and appear on the customer's original payment method within
5-7 business days. Refunds of $500 or more require manager approval before
processing.

## Refund window

Customers may request a prorated refund within 30 days of the charge date.
Annual subscriptions cancelled mid-term are refunded prorated on unused whole
months, minus a 10% administrative fee.

## Non-refundable items

Setup fees, custom integrations, and third-party marketplace purchases are not
refundable. Chargebacks should be avoided; direct refunds resolve faster and
cost the company less in dispute fees.

## Processing

All refunds must be logged in the CRM with the reason code REFUND-DUP,
REFUND-WINDOW, or REFUND-GOODWILL. Customers receive a confirmation email when
the refund is issued.
