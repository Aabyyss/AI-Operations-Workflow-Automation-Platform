Where this comparison says we lose. At these inputs the pipeline comes out cheaper than all 4 priced plans. That is not a claim about the vendors' products, and it is not a claim about quality — it is what the meters do: per-outcome pricing charges you for the vendor's success, per-decision pricing charges you for the work. Change the volume or the billed-resolution share above and this can flip. The crossover rate is where it flips.

How the pipeline's number was produced

  • indicative — from 7 recorded decision(s) in this deployment's run ledger ($0.00018 per decision), in mock mode. Floor for calling it representative: 20 decisions.
  • Every ticket is one decision, including the 429/month that go to a human — that is the honest cost of the work, not only the cost of the wins.
  • Risk gates are deterministic and tested: refunds ≥ a configured limit, security-controlled actions, weak retrieval and low confidence always reach a human queue. If the pipeline cannot substantiate a decision, it does not make it.

Assumptions

  • Vendor side: 1,500 tickets/month × 50.0% billed resolutions × the plan's per-outcome rate, plus 0 seats and any platform fee.
  • Our side: 1,500 tickets/month × $0.00018 measured cost per decision (every ticket, not only the resolved ones) + $50.0 infra allowance.
  • A per-outcome 'resolution' and a pipeline 'decision' are not guaranteed to be the same unit; we charge ourselves per decision, which is the conservative framing for us.
  • Market prices are a list-price snapshot taken 2026-10-07.
  • Human review time is excluded on both sides; our measured escalation rate is 28.6%, so set include_human_review_cost to price it.

Caveats — the parts that would otherwise flatter us

  • Cost per decision is indicative: 7 recorded decision(s), floor is 20. Run more tickets in this deployment before quoting the figure.
  • This deployment is in MOCK mode: token cost is deterministic and comparable run-to-run, but it is priced from synthetic token counts, not real vendor spend. Set AIOPS_MODE=live + OPENAI_API_KEY to compare real cost.
  • Neither side includes integration, migration or training effort, and enterprise contracts are usually quoted with volume commitments the list price hides.
  • Handoff labour is excluded: the vendor's unresolved tickets and our escalated ones both cost human minutes that this model does not bill to either side.
